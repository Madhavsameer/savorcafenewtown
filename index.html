A New Town kitchen for every craving

Savor Cafe' brings together the tandoor, the Chinese wok and the Bengali kitchen under one roof — a menu built for regular weeknight orders as much as weekend get-togethers.

From tandoori kebabs and butter chicken to Manchurian, chilli fish and mutton biryani, the menu spans over a hundred dishes — so the table rarely has to agree on just one cuisine.
